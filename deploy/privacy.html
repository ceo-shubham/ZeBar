Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | ZeBar by ZENSTRO

1. Overview and Ownership

ZeBar is a web-based utility for QR code creation and media hosting owned and operated by ZENSTRO (website: zenstro.space), under the direction of Founder and Chief Executive Officer Shubham Pathak. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, and managed when you access or use ZeBar.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to provide authentication, generate QR codes, and host user-submitted media:

  • Authentication Information: When signing in with Google, we collect your verified email address, full name, and Google profile identification to establish your user account and maintain your dashboard history.
  • User Content and Destinations: Text entries, destination URLs, labels, and color choices submitted during the generation of QR codes.
  • Media Files: Audio files (such as MP3) and image files that you choose to upload for distribution through QR codes.
  • System Audit Logs: Account registration timestamps, login records, and creation events recorded for security and auditing purposes.

3. Storage and Data Retention

Uploaded media assets are stored securely on private cloud object storage (Backblaze B2) and streamed through authenticated server endpoints. All QR code generation history and uploaded files are retained securely on our infrastructure for ongoing service delivery, safety, and anti-abuse verification.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising Policy

ZeBar does not display third-party advertisements. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or distribute your email address, QR destinations, or uploaded files to marketing networks, brokers, or external advertising platforms.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and restricted server-side credential management for cloud storage access.

6. Contact and Inquiries

For questions regarding your data or this policy, please reach out via zenstro.space.
